SUPPLEMENTARY DATA 1: MODEL PERFORMANCE

Data file: Supplementary_Data_1_model_performance.csv
Encoding: UTF-8, comma-separated, one header row; decimal point notation.
The CSV is preserved byte-for-byte from the approved submission data.

There are 156 records: 13 model algorithms by four urban-form types by three
random seeds. Twelve algorithms are global candidates. GWRF is the additional
geographically weighted comparator, not a thirteenth global candidate.
Figure 5 ranks the nine global algorithms retained after model screening.

Field definitions
Experiment_ID: identifier of the archived modelling run; the same run label
can cover multiple seeds and algorithms. It is not an observation identifier.
Seed: training/test split and model-fitting seed (42, 142 or 242).
Zone: urban-form identifier. Zone1 = Compact; Zone2 = Open mid-/high-rise;
Zone3 = Open low-rise; Zone4 = Green-space-dominated.
Zone_Label: English label corresponding to Zone.
Code: archived modelling-module identifier; retained for traceability.
Model: algorithm name.
Train_R2: coefficient of determination for the 70% training split.
Train_RMSE: training root mean squared error, degrees Celsius.
Train_MAE: training mean absolute error, degrees Celsius.
Test_R2: coefficient of determination for the 30% held-out split.
Test_RMSE: held-out root mean squared error, degrees Celsius.
Test_MAE: held-out mean absolute error, degrees Celsius.
Overfitting_Score: Train_R2 minus Test_R2, dimensionless.

The response is daytime land-surface temperature weighted by residents aged
65 years and older (PWP), in degrees Celsius. Type sample sizes are 646, 1,059,
660 and 1,710, respectively, totalling 4,075 analytical one-kilometre grids.
Seed values do not denote independent samples. Performance values are rounded
to three decimal places in this ledger, so displayed differences can contain
small rounding discrepancies. These are held-out performance records, not the
performance of explanatory models refitted to all observations.
